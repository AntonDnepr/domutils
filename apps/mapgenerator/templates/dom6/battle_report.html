Battle report parser

Turn screenshots of a Dominions 6 battle report into a table of units, kills and losses per nation, ready to copy as JSON.

How to get the best results
  1. Use the full-size battle report, not the small "Battle Summary" window. Bigger text is read more reliably.
  2. Take a normal screenshot (e.g. PrtSc / Win+Shift+S / Cmd+Shift+4). PNG is best. Don't photograph the screen with a phone and avoid heavily compressed JPEGs.
  3. Move the mouse pointer away from the table before taking the screenshot.
  4. Include the "Forces of …" header bar of every block. Rows above the first header in a screenshot are skipped, because the nation they belong to is unknown.
  5. Include the grey "Total" row. It is used to check that every unit was read.
  6. Long report? Take several screenshots, each starting at a "Forces of …" header, and add them all here. The results are merged.
  7. Crop each screenshot to the table: from just left of the unit names to just right of the dead column, from the header bar down to the Total row. Portraits and banners on the left may stay; cut away surrounding text and window frames.

"Province Defence" blocks are ignored. Dead counts like 17+9 are added up (26). Empty cells become null.
